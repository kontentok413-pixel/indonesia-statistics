Indonesia in Numbers — an independent atlas of Indonesian statistics, 2015–2024. Data: Statistics Indonesia (BPS), the World Bank's World Development Indicators, the United Nations, and Indonesia's Ministry of Health. Figures are the latest published vintage; where two official measures differ, both are shown and explained.

This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Statistics Indonesia (BPS) or the World Bank. It runs entirely from its own files and makes no network requests.
